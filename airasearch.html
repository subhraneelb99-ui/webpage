FAQ

Questions search firms ask us

Isn't this just Boolean behind a chat box?

No. Many "natural language" tools turn your sentence back into the same filters. AIRA Search plans its own search, asks a question when the brief is unclear, reads call transcripts and notes, and explains each match.

Our data is messy. Will it still work?

Yes. AIRA Search is built to read the messy parts, like notes, transcripts and emails. You don't need to clean or tag anything first.

Does it decide who goes to the client?

No. AIRA finds people and explains why they fit. Your consultant makes the call.

Does it search outside our database?

AIRA Search works on your firm's own records, enriched with external data such as funding information. To find brand-new people, use AIRA Source.

Which plan includes AIRA Search?

The AIRA Plan, which has custom pricing. Existing customers can ask their account manager about upgrading.

We use Bullhorn. How hard is it to switch?

Self-serve import is free. White-glove migration usually takes 6–8 weeks and is priced by data volume.
